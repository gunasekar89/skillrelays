Important: This is an independent research synthesis, not a prospectus issued by GCU. Published fees and offerings can change. The report deliberately separates direct evidence, institutional claims, independently hosted data, and unresolved items.

Evidence protocol. The strongest sources used here are university-published tables and 2026 NIRF submissions, plus the UGC historical list and QS I-GAUGE entry. Reviews are treated as anecdotal and platform-specific. An item appearing on an official webpage is not automatically proof of a current programme intake, current statutory council approval, or university-wide placement outcome.
Fee method. Programme tuition totals in this document are calculated by summing the instalments in the newer table near the bottom of the university domestic fee webpage. Older fee tables remain on the same page, and no explicit academic-year label is attached to the newer domestic table; therefore all totals are published indicative calculations, not confirmed 2026–27 quotations. Deposits, examinations, consumables, hostel, travel and personal expenses are generally excluded unless shown separately.

1. Executive summary and rapid verdict
Garden City University (GCU) is a private university in Bengaluru created under Karnataka State Act No. 47 of 2013, drawing on a Garden City education group founded in 1992. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD options across commerce, engineering, computing, life sciences, media, languages, health sciences and other subjects. The university operates an Old Madras Road campus and a Student Knowledge Park in the Hoskote area. [01][02]
• Legitimacy: UGC historical list explicitly includes Garden City University as a Karnataka private university;
this supports university-level identity, but does not establish every programme-specific professional approval.
[03]
• External rating: QS I-GAUGE lists an overall Gold rating; it is a rating and should not be confused with a QS World University Ranking place. [15][16]

• NAAC caution: the university’s own history attaches the historic NAAC A grade to Garden City College of Science and Management Studies, not unambiguously to a currently accredited Garden City University. Request GCU’s own current NAAC certificate and validity. [02]
• Published MBA management outcomes: NIRF 2026 management submission gives 90 placed of 93 graduates for academic year 2024–25, ₹7.50 lakh median package and 3 proceeding to higher education. These numbers are not all-course or engineering-wide results. [11]
• Cost: official domestic tuition table lists about ₹14.40 lakh for some four-year B.Tech/BE streams, ₹5.94 lakh for MBA and ₹3.32 lakh for MCA (calculated totals, excluding extras). [05]
• Research: PhD pathways include part-time and full-time; the PhD page lists an exam, research presentation and interview process. [24] Who should consider GCU?
A prospective student can consider GCU when a particular course’s curriculum, individual faculty and labs, location, affordability, and written placement evidence meet their goals. A decision should be course-specific.
For high-cost professional programmes, independent verification of the exact course approval, graduating cohort outcomes and complete four-year budget is essential.

Placement process and employers
GCU describes placement coordination through its Industry Institute Partnership Cell, campus recruitment drives, a job fair called “Launch,” a placement/ERP module that distributes relevant job descriptions, skills training and an official recruiters gallery. A recruiter’s logo signals claimed historical contact, not a guarantee of current on-campus hiring or number of offers. [13][14] Careers360 cites names including Google, Cognizant, Axis Bank, Biocon, Deloitte, NDTV, LG, Paytm, Infosys and Siemens as recruiter examples. Treat these as third-party directory claims unless supported by company-wise current offers, hire counts and role/salary breakdowns. [19] What constitutes robust placement evidence?
• A programme-by-programme audited table: admitted, eligible, opted in, placed, higher studies, off-campus outcomes and unmatched graduates.
• Median rather than only highest package; fixed compensation separated from variable pay and internships.
• Last three cohorts of data and named employer / role / compensation distributions (redacted appropriately).
• Distinction between a company recruiting any university student and recruiting in the target course.
Budget and return-on-investment illustration
Consider a published four-year B.Tech CSE tuition of ₹14.40 lakh. Adding eight engineering semester exams at ₹3,500 (₹28,000), registration ₹2,000 and caution deposit ₹5,000 yields about ₹14.75 lakh cash budget before any hostel, transport, laptop, food or personal costs. This is not a full cost of attendance and is only valid if the listed fee schedule remains unchanged. On-campus housing could substantially increase the total. [05][07]


9. PhD and research routes
GCU advertises doctoral study in Management, Journalism, Hospitality Management, Fashion & Apparel Design, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Forensic Science, English, Computational Sciences & IT, Psychology and Physiotherapy. The separate research/admissions flyer also lists electronics engineering and computer science engineering, but the live homepage doctoral menu differs. Verify supervisor approvals and field-specific availability directly. [01][24][25] Doctoral selection and working-professional considerations
• Published stages: research entrance exam (Criteria I), supervisor interaction, proposal presentation (Criteria II), personal interview (Criteria III), composite merit and provisional admission. [24]
• Published eligibility: 55% / 5.5 CGPA master’s threshold with defined relaxation categories. [24]
• The connected 2025–26 flyer says entrance examination of 100 marks, 50% research aptitude and 50% subject expertise, 120-minute multiple-choice format, and mentions NET exemptions subject to its 2024 UGC reference. These flyer details may be outdated for 2026–27. [25]
• The same old flyer requests an employer NOC for employed candidates and mentions department work obligations. These must be reconfirmed against current regulations and working-professional PhD policy. [25]
• Request written confirmation of part-time residency requirements, daytime/on-campus attendance, research ethics approval, conference/publication expectations, supervisor capacity, cloud/computing lab infrastructure and fees beyond minimum registration.

Recommended action before paying an admission deposit
• Ask for the correct academic-year admission prospectus in PDF and a fee quote with a university-authorized seal or verifiable official email trail.
• Check exact award nomenclature, duration, classroom campus, eligibility, regulatory conditions and recognized modes of delivery.
• Collect cohort-level placement details from the exact department for the last three years.
• Ask for written refund rules, caution deposit treatment, hostel and food tariffs, transport routes and examination fees.
• Meet academic staff; assess lab access and attendance policy; speak with students independent of marketing staff.
• For research students, confirm a named supervisor, research infrastructure, employer NOC requirement, ethics and a written realistic completion plan.
Assessment: GCU appears to be a legally established private university with wide programme offerings and an independent Gold QS I-GAUGE rating. Its published information is broad, but some admissions and financial pages mix current and outdated details. A decision should be made only after verification of the specific programme, up-to-date written charges and programme-specific outcomes.
